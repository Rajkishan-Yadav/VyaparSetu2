Digital Payments

Learn how small vendors can safely accept and verify digital payments using UPI and QR codes.

₹

1. UPI Basics

UPI (Unified Payments Interface) allows customers to transfer money directly from their bank account to the vendor's bank account using a UPI app.

How to receive a UPI payment?

  1. Ask the customer to open their UPI app.
  2. Customer enters your UPI ID or scans your QR code.
  3. Customer enters the payment amount.
  4. Customer completes the payment.
  5. Verify that the payment was successfully received.
▦

3. Payment Verification

Always verify the payment before giving goods or services to the customer.

Verification checklist

  • Check your UPI app or bank notification.
  • Confirm the received amount.
  • Check the transaction status.
  • Do not depend only on a customer screenshot.
  • Give the goods only after confirming payment.
!

4. Safety & Fraud Awareness

Digital payments are convenient, but vendors should be careful about common payment scams.

Important Safety Rules

  • Never share your UPI PIN.
  • Never share your OTP with anyone.
  • Never share your bank password.
  • Do not trust payment screenshots without verification.
  • Remember: you normally don't enter your UPI PIN to receive money.

Use Digital Payments Safely

Accept digital payments confidently and always verify the transaction before completing a sale.
